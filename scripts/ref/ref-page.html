01

Angkas↗

Rebuilding a national ride-hailing platform without stopping the ride.

Angkas is the Philippines' leading motorcycle taxi platform, running two apps, one for passengers and one for partner riders, moving millions of people through some of the worst traffic on earth.

Operating at scale

  • 4M+users supported
  • 20K+partner riders
  • 2production apps

The challenge

The two apps had evolved independently, with different architecture, security approaches, delivery workflows, and implementation patterns. Maintenance cost kept climbing, and shipping consistently across both products got harder as the platform grew.

What I did

  • Established a shared mobile architecture across both apps, standardizing security, CI/CD, design patterns, networking, state management, observability, and engineering conventions.
  • That foundation let us rebuild and progressively ship both applications within six months while the existing platform stayed operational.

The result

  • Lower crash rate
  • Faster, safer releases
  • Shared architecture
  • Consistent CI/CD
  • Reduced duplication

02

Gold Rank Review Center↗

Cutting a seven-figure streaming bill down to size.

Gold Rank Review Center prepares over a thousand students for their board exams through a digital learning platform spanning assessments, review materials, video, and structured study experiences across web and mobile.

The challenge

The existing platform struggled under concurrent usage, with recurring stability issues affecting the student experience. Video delivery had also become disproportionately expensive, with annual streaming costs reaching seven figures.

What I did

  • Re-architected the platform to handle concurrent usage reliably across web and mobile.
  • Redesigned the video delivery strategy and infrastructure, reducing annual streaming costs from seven figures to approximately ₱100K.
  • Delivered the platform across web and mobile on schedule.

The result

  • Streaming cost cut over 90%, from seven figures to about ₱100K a year
  • Stable under production load
  • Web and mobile delivered on schedule
